Rules

  • Traffic-light results. Every result is green when it’s good, amber at the limit and red in danger. Tap one to see the three levels spelled out. Better than usual (more steps, higher HRV) stays green.
  • Two colour layers, never mixed. Green, amber and red judge a result. The living gradient sets the mood. Orange marks today only.
  • One big number per screen. Light-weight numerals, generous air, everything else secondary.
  • The gradient is data. Calm sand and lavender when recovered, saturated ember when strained, faster when your heart is, grainier when stressed, dusk after bedtime. It breathes at your sleeping breathing rate.
  • One type family. Geist on the web, SF Pro on iPhone; weight and size carry the hierarchy.
  • Respect stillness. With Reduce Motion on, the gradient holds still and only changes colour.
